Cragpack system

Rules

  1. Orange is for doing: actions, ticks, “now” and progress. Never a warning.
  2. A dashed outline means missing or not done yet.
  3. Offline dot: full = saved · half = partly saved · ring = saving · empty = not saved. ↓ is only the Download button.
  4. One primary action per screen, in the dock.
  5. Tap targets 44px or more (52px default). Text 13px or more.
  6. Grades: the class is “g” + the grade’s first digit (.g1 – .g9). Font for boulders (1 – 9A), French for routes (1a – 9c). V grades take the class of their Font equivalent. 1–2 and 3–4 share a soft colour.
  7. Photo surfaces stay dark and the recap sheet stays light, in both themes.
  8. Safe areas: content starts below the status bar and the dock sits above the home indicator (--safe-top, --safe-bottom, the OS insets in the app). Scrolling lists end with --dock-space.
  9. Icons are Lucide (icons.svg), never text symbols.
  10. A sheet for choices and short forms, a dialog only for what can’t be undone, a toast to offer Undo.
  11. Danger red is only for delete, remove and leave.
  12. Local first: planning needs no account. The account prompt opens only to invite, join, share a link or sync devices; local trips move into the new account.
  13. New screens use only these classes. A new value starts as a token in cragpack.css and cragpack.tokens.json.

Screen anatomy

  1. Top bar: back or ✕ · status pill · one icon action.
  2. Title (.h1), or a photo hero with .title-xl.
  3. Content: .row lists, .card tiles, .field forms.
  4. Dock: tab bar inside a trip, one call to action in a flow, log actions on a boulder or route.
  5. Forms and flows close with x at top left; drill-downs go back with chevron-left.
